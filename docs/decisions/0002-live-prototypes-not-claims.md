# Decision 0002: Live prototypes, not invented project claims

Date: 2026-09-04

## Decision

Add future-facing interactive work as functioning browser experiments and label it clearly as such. Do not imply that a separate research project, dataset, paper, or production deployment already exists.

## Why

- A working small demonstration is stronger evidence of understanding than a decorative fictional chart.
- Clear labels preserve trust with technical hiring managers.
- The prototype can later become a standalone repository without rewriting the portfolio narrative.

## Applied example

The live ML playground trains a real small multilayer perceptron on deterministic synthetic data. Its displayed loss and accuracy are calculated in the visitor's browser and are explicitly described as educational in-sample results, not benchmark claims.

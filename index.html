05 — EVIDENCE, NOT DECORATION

Every claim needs a trail.

Explore how I structure research so another person can inspect the choices, reproduce the run, and find the limits.

01

Control the experiment

Versioned configurations, fixed seeds, documented datasets, and leakage-aware splits make the comparison interpretable.

config → seed → split → run
02

Measure more than accuracy

Latency, throughput, memory, model size, FLOPs, and energy proxies reveal trade-offs hidden by a single headline metric.

quality × cost × constraints
03

Study the failures

Confusion matrices, per-class metrics, drift signals, and hard examples show where the system stops being dependable.

average ≠ guarantee
04

Export with verification

Cross-runtime parity checks and deployment reports keep the artifact connected to the model that was evaluated.

export → compare → approve
